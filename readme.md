I want to make plans for an app. The purpose is to help people fix their body aches. 


### Target audience:
- 30+ year olds
- White collar / desk workers who are mostly sedentary

### Background thought
- is that as we get older our bodies start to fall apart or just not heal anymore. So small injuries that used to resolve on their own, just never heal. The older you get, the more of them you accumulate. Also, they tend to get worse over time and not better

### Why
- Body: as we age we stop healing as well
- Circumstance: working at a desk all day means we don't move. Then, for fun many of us either sit at the same desk for video games, or on the couch. Again, not really moving. 
- We stop moving our bodies in a many ways, so certain muslces just don't even get used anymore.

### What
- I want an app to help me reverse this for myself. 
- I have done some PT over the years, but I find it hard to keep track of 
  - what exercise to do
  - what cadence to do them
  - which muscle groups overlap when sometimes i want to do a PT exercise but I also enjoy other "regular" exercise
  - which exercises are hard/easy and what kind of intensity should be used for each
  - What kind of health/body issues I've had and which exercises seemed to help repair it
  - Or, if I tried exercies and they seemed to make it worse
- I want it available on my phone
- I want to have visuals of the exercises so it's easy to remember. Not sure if these can be 3D created

## Current Outstanding questions

- platform: i don't know if I should/can use flutter to make an app. Or if I have to go more intense and use Unity to make 3D rendering of the exercises so it feels more imersive - or like you have to get your little pet to do the exercises with you.
- Where do I source these exercises from?
- How do we know what muscle groups these are good for?
- How do you know which exercises to pick from?

## Overarching Goal
This is not an app to get in the best shape of your life. I want to help keep people functionally fit. I want to help increase healthspan (notjust lifespan)
